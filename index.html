Who we are

About Anantha Poolla Foundation

A community impact organization turning collective action into measurable change — making history while making a difference.

Our story

Built on showing up.

Anantha Poolla Foundation began with a simple belief: that everyday people, acting together, can solve problems that feel too big to face alone. From food relief to health camps to keeping children in school, we turn compassion into action on the ground.

The Mega Charity Run 2026 is our boldest chapter yet — uniting 5,000 people for an official India Book of Records attempt, with every rupee funding four real causes.

Mission

Action over intention

To deliver direct, measurable support across food, health, hygiene and education for those who need it most.

Vision

Compassion at scale

A community where collective participation makes lasting change ordinary, not exceptional.

Values

Trust & transparency

Every contribution is accounted for, and impact is reported back to the community we serve.
